Kept only so old dashboards can be explained. Do not use in new reporting.

## Definition
Legacy churn counted a venue as churned after 30 days without a login by any staff user, even if the venue still processed paid orders through the POS.

## Why it was retired
Login inactivity overstated churn during Ramadan hours changes and during weeks when managers used device PINs instead of individual logins.
