## Definition
Repeat order rate is the share of paying consumer accounts with two or more captured orders in the selected period, divided by paying consumer accounts with at least one captured order in the same period.

## Notes
Guest checkout orders without an account are excluded. A venue-level repeat rate is not defined; do not average venue rates to get a network rate.
